About the brand

nikw.co is the portfolio of Nik Williamson, a design systems engineer in Orlando, FL.

A polished design-systems voice with a soft, physical sense of play. Tokens behave like jelly, and related things melt together.

Principles

Three rules every page, component and scene follows.

Polished first

Crisp type, generous spacing and strict contrast. The playfulness only works because the base is exact.

Goo means relation

Things merge only when they are connected, like a token and its alias. Unrelated content stays crisp and separate.

Color is a fill

Lime, orange and pink are surfaces you put ink on. Grape is the only accent that can be text.

Color tones

Each fill has a nine-step scale for hover states, tinted surfaces and charts. The 500 step is the brand color itself.

Color pairings

Every fill with every brand color that takes the same text color: lime, orange and white carry ink, grape and ink carry white. Pink appears only as goo. Cards are grouped by fill; the goo takes the second color, so text reads the same over both.

Contrast

Every text pairing is checked against WCAG 2.2. The ratios here are calculated live on the page.

  • Text on lime, orange or pink is always grape ink.
  • Lime and orange are never text on the ground.
  • Grape is the only accent used as text.

The spring

One underdamped spring for anything that responds to the user. The small overshoot is what reads as squish.

Interaction

Hover and focus pull droplets out of a shape and let them snap back. Pointer-driven blobs ease toward the cursor and stretch along their direction of travel.

Transition

Page changes are made of goo blobs in a single color. Wash closes in from every side and becomes the next page. Melt drips down over the page, swaps it while it's covered, and oozes off the bottom.

Reduced motion

When someone asks for reduced motion, shapes keep their goo but stop moving. State changes happen instantly, transitions swap the page without a sweep, and nothing drifts or follows the pointer.

Multicolor goo

Goo is a blur followed by an alpha threshold, so shapes fuse when they get close. Text never goes inside the filter. Blobs sit behind or beside headlines, never over body copy.

Move your pointer through the field. The lime blob follows on the spring.

Solid goo

One fill, one mass. Use it when the shape itself is the point, like a hero backdrop or a section transition. Grape is the default; lime and orange work on the same rules.

Move your pointer through the field. The cursor blob joins the mass on the spring.

Multicolor solid goo

Several fills, each its own mass. Blobs only fuse with blobs of the same color; different colors slide over and under each other without merging, so every color keeps a clean edge.

Move your pointer through the field. The lime cursor blob joins the lime goo and passes over the rest.

Decoration

Decoration is a puddle: one blob with children around it, each trailing a short chain. Centred near a corner it reads as a corner; scaled up and pushed off a side it reads as an edge. Every puddle is drawn fresh for each surface, so no two share a shape.

  • One decoration per surface, in one color from the pairings.
  • Keep it clear of the wordmark and of body text.
  • Corners and edges live on posts and section dividers.

Digital application

The portfolio homepage: wordmark top left, one squishy headline, and work cards in brand fills. White cards on lilac, no borders or shadows.
